About.

I'm Brian Ahuma Kabutey, but most people just call me Ahuma. I'm a developer who finds joy in writing code that solves real problems and building interfaces that feel intuitive.

When I'm not at my desk, you'll likely find me with a violin. Music and code share more in common than most people think. Both demand precision, creativity, and the patience to debug until things sound (or work) exactly right.

I believe in clean, maintainable code and designs that don't get in the way. Less noise, more substance.
